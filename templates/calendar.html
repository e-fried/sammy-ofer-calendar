Add to Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar in a desktop browser.
  2. Next to Other calendars, click +, then From URL.
  3. Paste the subscription URL and click Add calendar.

For Outlook, use Add calendar → Subscribe from web. For Apple Calendar, use File → New Calendar Subscription.

The service checks the stadium schedule every six hours when its worker is running. Your calendar app controls its own refresh timing. This is a separate calendar; it does not automatically reserve time on your primary work calendar. Set and verify notifications in your calendar app.
